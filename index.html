FORMAT

36 entrants · 72 teams · 16 scoring fixtures

Each entrant has been allocated one Champions League (UCL) club and one Europa League (UEL) club. Each club will contest eight league-phase fixtures between September and January, providing every entrant with 16 scoring fixtures in total. Only league-phase matches are included in the competition.

＋
SCORING

Goals scored + goals conceded

For every fixture, all goals scored and all goals conceded by an entrant's allocated club are added to that entrant's total. For example, a 2-2 draw contributes 4 goals. The Champions League (UCL) and Europa League (UEL) totals are combined, and the entrant with the highest aggregate total after all 16 fixtures is the winner. Standings are updated as results are recorded.
